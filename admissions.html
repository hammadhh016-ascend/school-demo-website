Admissions

Applying to Riverstone.

We admit students in grades K–8 on a rolling basis, with most decisions made for fall enrollment between January and March.

How it works

Five steps from first visit to enrollment.

Schedule a tour

Weekday tours run 8:30–10:00 AM during the school year. Our fall Open House on October 17 is a good low-pressure first step.

Submit an application

Complete the online application, including a family statement and, for grades 3 and up, a short student questionnaire.

Records & recommendation

We request a current report card and a brief recommendation from your child's current teacher.

Student visit

Applicants for grades 1–8 spend a half-day in a Riverstone classroom; kindergarten applicants join a small playgroup assessment.

Decision & enrollment

Families are notified by mid-March and have two weeks to accept a spot and submit the enrollment contract and deposit.

Tuition & financial aid

2026–27 school year.

Tuition

Tuition is $24,600 for kindergarten through grade 5 and $27,200 for grades 6–8, inclusive of books, technology, and most field trips.

Financial aid

Roughly 22% of Riverstone families receive need-based aid, awarded on a sliding scale. Aid applications are due at the same time as admissions applications.

Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most from prospective families.

When should we apply?

Applications for fall enrollment open in October and are reviewed on a rolling basis, with priority given to complete applications received by January 15.

Do you require standardized testing?

We do not require standardized entrance testing for kindergarten through grade 2. Applicants for grades 3 and up complete a short, low-stakes assessment during their visit day.

Is transportation provided?

Riverstone runs two bus routes covering central and north Fairview. Families outside these routes typically carpool or drive directly.

Can we apply mid-year?

Mid-year applications are considered when space is available. Contact the admissions office directly to check current openings by grade.

What is a typical class size?

Classes average 16 students, with two teachers in kindergarten through grade 2 and one lead teacher plus support in grades 3–8.

Ready to take the next step?

Start with a tour, or reach out with questions before applying.

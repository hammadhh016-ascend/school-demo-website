Student Life

What fills the afternoon at Riverstone.

Athletics, arts, and clubs aren't extras here — they're where students practice teamwork, performance, and follow-through.

Athletics

Interscholastic teams begin in grade 5.

Fall

Soccer, cross country, and field hockey for grades 5–8, with intramural options for grades 3–4.

Winter

Basketball and indoor track, plus a beginner ski program on Friday afternoons.

Spring

Baseball, softball, and track & field, with an all-school field day closing out the season.

Arts & performances

Three major productions each year.

November

Fall Concert

Every grade performs, from kindergarten songs to the middle school band and choir.

February

Winter Play

A student-cast production, open to grades 4–8, with sets built in the design wing.

May

Spring Art Show

A gallery night showcasing a year of student work across every grade and medium.

What sets the day apart

Three commitments that shape every classroom.

Academics

Depth over pace

Teachers slow down on the ideas that matter, so students build real understanding instead of rushing to the next unit.

Community

Every student known

With an 8:1 ratio, advisors track each child's progress closely and loop families in before small issues grow.

Character

Responsibility, practiced daily

From classroom jobs to student-led conferences, children take on real responsibility from kindergarten onward.

Academics

A curriculum that builds year over year.

Every subject is sequenced across nine grades, so skills learned in kindergarten are still being used — and deepened — in eighth grade.

Curriculum by division

What a typical week looks like at each level.

K–2

Lower School

Phonics-based reading, hands-on math, and daily outdoor time. Homeroom teachers stay with the same class most of the day.

3–5

Middle Elementary

Students begin rotating between subject-specialist teachers for math, science, and humanities, with a research project each spring.

6–8

Middle School

A full departmentalized schedule, a foreign language requirement, and an independent capstone project in eighth grade.

Signature programs

Where Riverstone spends extra time and resources.

STEM & design

Every grade has weekly time in the design wing — from simple machines in first grade to a robotics elective in eighth.

World languages

Spanish begins in kindergarten; French is added as an option in sixth grade, taught by native-speaking faculty.

Visual & performing arts

All students take studio art and music through eighth grade, with a fall play and spring concert each year.

Learning support

A part-time learning specialist works with classroom teachers to support students who need extra scaffolding or challenge.

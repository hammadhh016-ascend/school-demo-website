About Riverstone

Nearly forty years of small classes and serious work.

Riverstone was founded on a simple idea: children do their best thinking when they're known well. That idea still shapes every decision we make.

Our mission

Riverstone Day School prepares children in grades K–8 to think clearly, work diligently, and treat others with care. We believe rigorous academics and a genuinely happy school day are not in tension — they depend on each other.

We keep classes small, hire teachers who love their subjects, and give students real ownership of their work, from first-grade classroom jobs to eighth-grade capstone projects.

A short history

Riverstone opened in 1987 in a converted farmhouse with 26 students and three teachers. Over four decades we've grown to 310 students across two connected buildings, while keeping the ratio that made the school work in the first place.

Today's campus includes a library, a science and design wing added in 2016, and eleven acres of fields and gardens used daily for outdoor learning.

Campus & facilities

Eleven acres, two connected buildings.

Library & commons

A full-time librarian, 9,000 volumes, and a reading room used for morning meeting.

Science & design wing

Wet labs for grades 4–8 and a maker space with tools for wood, textiles, and electronics.

Fields & gardens

Two playing fields, a running track, and a student-run vegetable garden used by every grade.

Accreditation & affiliations

Riverstone Day School is accredited by the Association of Independent Schools and is a member of the National Association of Independent Schools (NAIS) and the Fairview Council of Schools.
